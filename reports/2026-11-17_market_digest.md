- Nasdaq **24,509.76 (-2.49%)** underperformed, so this was led by tech and growth stocks. Dow -1.41% ([Motley Fool/Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nov-16-222502139.html)).

- Implication: this was a selloff in the AI/chip trade, not a broad global macro shock. Europe's resilience shows money rotating out of tech rather than fleeing equities wholesale.
