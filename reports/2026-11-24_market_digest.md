## 1. Headline & Sentiment
- S&P 500 closed at **7,237.17 (+4.73%)** on Mon 23 Nov. The Nasdaq rose **+6.51%** and the VIX fell **-25.83% to 26.62**. The S&P had fallen for seven straight sessions and closed in correction on Friday; Monday ended that streak. Both indices had their best day since April 2025 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-november-23-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-surge-after-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-182039397.html)).
- **Read: bullish for the near term, but this is a relief rally, not a confirmed bottom.** The index is still below both EMAs, and the VIX is above 25.
- **Biggest driver:** Nvidia's board reinstated **Jensen Huang as CEO**. He had resigned on 12 Nov. The board said it had "received input from senior levels of the United States government" after President Trump publicly urged it to rehire him. NVDA closed **+31%**, its biggest gain since 2003 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-november-23-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-surge-after-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-182039397.html)).

## 7. Technical Analysis
- **Close 7,237.17** vs **EMA-20 7,426.71 (-2.55%)** and **EMA-200 7,393.06 (-2.11%)**. The index is still **below both averages**, even after a 4.7% gain.
- **Regime:** EMA-20 is still above EMA-200, which the raw report flags as bullish. But the gap is only about 34 points and closing quickly. A **bearish EMA-20/EMA-200 crossover** is possible within days unless the rebound continues.
- **Resistance:** 7,393 (EMA-200), then 7,427 (EMA-20). This 7,390–7,430 band is the key test. On 16 Nov the index closed at 7,416.59, within this band, so the area was support before the breakdown.
- **Support:** 7,152.66 (Monday's low). Below that, Friday's correction low (not in the raw file).
- **Pattern:** an oversold capitulation was followed by a one-day upside reversal on very heavy volume (S&P 5.61bn). Rallies of 4% or more in one day usually happen inside downtrends, not at confirmed bottoms. The rebound needs **a close back above the EMA-200** to count as more than a bear-market rally.
